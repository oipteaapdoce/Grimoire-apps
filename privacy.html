Privacy Policy — Grimoire Companion

Effective date: October 15, 2026
Published by Enochian Alchemical Magick (www.enochian.org)

The short version

Grimoire Companion collects no data about you. Nothing you do in the app ever leaves your device.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no tracking, no crash reporting, and no advertising. It does not read your contacts or files. It works fully offline — once installed, it needs no network connection at all.

Location

Planetary hours and stellar timing depend on where you are, so the app can use your device location — only if you ask it to. When you tap the "Use my location" button, the app reads your current latitude and longitude once, fills in the latitude/longitude fields, and computes your local timing on your device. Your location is never transmitted, stored, or shared with us or anyone else. You can also enter latitude and longitude manually and never grant location access at all.

Links to our website

The app contains links to enochian.org (courses, talismans, and ritual services). If you tap one of those links, your browser opens our website, which is governed by that site's own privacy practices — but the app itself still sends us nothing.

Children

Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects no personal information from children either.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address.

Contact

For privacy questions, reach us through the contact page at www.enochian.org.
