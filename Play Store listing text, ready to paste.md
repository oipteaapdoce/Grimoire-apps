## Full description
Grimoire Companion is a study reference for students of the Western grimoire tradition.

SPIRIT FINDER — Browse four complete traditions:
• The 72 spirits of the Goetia, with traditional seals in the 1904 Crowley–Mathers style
• Grimorium Verum — all 44 spirits, from the three superiors through Duke Syrach's eighteen and Sergulath's subordinates
• The Grand Grimoire — Lucifuge Rofocale and the infernal hierarchy, cross-linked to their Goetic entries
• The 7 Olympic Spirits of the Arbatel, with native planets and weekdays wired into timing
Filter by tradition, or search by intent — wealth, protection, love, dreams, and more.

PLANETARY HOURS — A traditional unequal planetary-hours calculator for timing ritual work.

ENOCHIAN ANGELS — A quick reference to the Enochian angels.

Built by Enochian Alchemical Magick (enochian.org), with links to courses, talismans, and ritual services for deeper study.

The app works fully offline and collects no data — no accounts, no tracking, no ads.
